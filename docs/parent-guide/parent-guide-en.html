Your child uses the app in class and at home. You do not need to log in — a weekly report arrives by email.

How you can help

  • Ask to see “Today’s plan” each day. Ten minutes daily beats one long session.
  • Praise the streak and the effort, not only the score.
  • Check that Homework shows “Submitted” before the next lesson.
  • Read Teacher Feedback together and ask your child to explain one corrected question.

Your weekly report

Every week you will receive an email with lessons, homework, online practice (days practised, accuracy, study time) and textbook progress, in English or Korean.

If something will not load

Wait a few seconds, then tap the refresh button. If it keeps happening, tell your child’s teacher.
