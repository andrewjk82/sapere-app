# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 3: Curve sketching and transformations

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, Chapter 3.

## Core methods
- Sketch a curve from its intercepts, turning points, symmetry, domain, range, and asymptotes.
- A horizontal dilation by factor k replaces x with x/k inside the original function. A vertical translation is added outside the function.
- For reciprocal graphs, denominator zeros become vertical asymptotes; identify sign, horizontal asymptotes, and turning behaviour on each interval.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not place a horizontal translation or dilation outside the function argument.
- For reciprocal graphs, preserve domain exclusions and check whether an absolute value reflects negative branches upward.

## Diagram requirements
- Plot calculated intercepts and turning points before joining them with smooth branches.
- Show asymptotes as dashed lines and keep each branch on its correct interval.
