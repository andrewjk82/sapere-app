# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 5
## Geometry review

### Standard solution methodology
Angles on a straight line sum to 180 degrees; co-interior angles between parallel lines sum to 180 degrees; interior angles of a triangle sum to 180 degrees.

### Common student pitfalls
Using corresponding/alternate rules when the diagram marks co-interior angles; placing an angle label outside its marked sector.
