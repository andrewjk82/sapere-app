# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 4 — Integration

## Core concepts
- Reverse chain rule: \(\int f'(x)[f(x)]^n dx=[f(x)]^{n+1}/(n+1)+C\), with the inner derivative accounted for.
- Definite integrals give signed area; geometric area below the axis is counted positively by reversing the sign.
- Split compound regions at roots/intersections before evaluating area.
- Use substitution in definite integrals with careful bound changes, or retain x-bounds and substitute an antiderivative.

## Standard methodology
1. Find intercepts and determine where the curve is above/below the axis.
2. Write the area as a sum/difference of definite integrals.
3. Find an antiderivative and show endpoint substitution explicitly.
4. For transformed functions, map key x-coordinates and account for orientation/reversal in the integral.

## Pitfalls
- A signed integral is not always the geometric area.
- Reversing integration limits changes the sign.
- Do not omit the constant when giving an indefinite integral.
