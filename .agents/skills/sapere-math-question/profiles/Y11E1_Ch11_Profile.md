# Y11 Extension 1 — Chapter 11: Polynomials

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 11 (2nd ed.), sections 11D–11E.

## Core methods
- Factor Theorem: `(x-a)` is a factor exactly when `P(a)=0`; for `(x+c)`, test `P(-c)`.
- A repeated root `a` satisfies both `P(a)=0` and `P'(a)=0`. Confirm multiplicity by factorisation or derivative.

## Common pitfalls
- Substitute the root with its correct sign.
- A root is not necessarily repeated; verify the derivative condition.
