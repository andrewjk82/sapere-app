# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 9 — Statistical analysis

## Core concepts
- Pearson correlation coefficient describes direction/strength of linear association and lies in \([-1,1]\).
- Least-squares regression line \(y=mx+c\) predicts y from x; inverse prediction solves the fitted equation for x.
- Quartiles and IQR describe spread; outlier fences are \(Q_1-1.5IQR\) and \(Q_3+1.5IQR\).

## Standard methodology
1. Identify explanatory and response variables from the question.
2. Compute/report the statistic to the requested precision and interpret its sign/magnitude.
3. Substitute into the regression equation for prediction; if predicting x from y, rearrange the fitted equation.
4. For outliers, find quartiles, calculate IQR and apply both fences.

## Pitfalls
- Correlation does not prove causation.
- Do not reverse explanatory and response variables in a regression line.
- Use the correct rounding and units for the requested prediction.
