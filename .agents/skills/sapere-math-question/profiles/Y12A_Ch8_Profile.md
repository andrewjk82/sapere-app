# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 8 — Series and finance

## Core concepts
- Arithmetic series: \(S_n=rac n2(2a+(n-1)d)\).
- Compound interest: \(A=P(1+i)^n\).
- Future value of end-of-period annuity payments: \(FV=M((1+i)^n-1)/i\).
- Loan repayment with equal payments: \(M=Pi/(1-(1+i)^{-n})\); total interest is total repayments minus principal.

## Standard methodology
1. Convert annual rates to the payment period and count the number of periods.
2. Choose the correct sequence/annuity/loan model and identify each variable.
3. Substitute values with units and calculate before rounding currency to cents.
4. Check payment timing (ordinary annuity: end of period) and interpret the result.

## Pitfalls
- Match rate period to payment period.
- Do not confuse present value with future value factors.
- For an AP total, solve for an integer day count and ensure the cumulative total is strictly greater than the target.
