# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 5 — Linear Equations and Inequalities

## Core concepts used in this batch
- Equations with brackets: expand both sides before collecting variable terms.
- Equations involving fractions: clear denominators by multiplying every term by the lowest common denominator, then use inverse operations.

## Solution method
1. Record the original equation and identify brackets or denominators.
2. Expand brackets or multiply every term by the common denominator.
3. Collect variable terms on one side and constants on the other, showing each operation on both sides.
4. Divide by the coefficient of the variable and substitute the result back to check.

## Common pitfalls
- Multiplying only some terms when clearing denominators.
- Losing a negative sign when expanding or moving a term.
- Performing an operation on only one side of an equation.
- Giving an unchecked value that does not satisfy the original equation.
