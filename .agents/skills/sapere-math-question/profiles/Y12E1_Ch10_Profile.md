# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 10: Projectile motion

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, sections 10A–10B.

## Core methods
- Resolve initial velocity into horizontal and vertical components. Apply constant-acceleration equations with consistent axes and gravity sign.
- Eliminate time to obtain the trajectory; use the vertex or differentiation for maximum height.
- To find impact with a slope, solve the trajectory and slope equations together, then convert horizontal displacement to distance along the slope if required.

## Common pitfalls
- Keep units and axis directions consistent.
- Distance along an incline is not the same as horizontal displacement.
