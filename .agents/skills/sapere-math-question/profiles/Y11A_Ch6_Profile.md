# Chapter Profile: Year 11 Advanced Chapter 6 — Trigonometry

## Core concepts
- In a right triangle, identify opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse relative to the marked angle; use sine, cosine, or tangent accordingly.
- For a general angle in quadrant II, sine is positive while cosine and tangent are negative. Use \(\sin^2	heta+\cos^2	heta=1\) and \(	an	heta=\sin	heta/\cos	heta\).
- For two observation points on level ground, set a common height and express horizontal distances using \(	an	heta=	ext{opposite}/	ext{adjacent}\).

## Standard methodology
1. Mark the angle, known lengths, and requested length on a labelled sketch.
2. Choose a ratio from the sides relative to the angle and write it before substitution.
3. For two observations, express both distances to the same vertical object and use the stated separation.
4. Solve algebraically, retain units, and check the result is positive and consistent with the diagram.

## Pitfalls
- The opposite and adjacent sides depend on which angle is being used.
- In quadrant II, do not make cosine positive.
- For two observations, the nearer point has the larger angle of elevation; do not reverse the 600 m separation.
