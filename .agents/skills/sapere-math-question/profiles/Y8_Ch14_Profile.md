# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 14
## Circles

### Standard solution methodology
Circle area A=πr² and circumference C=2πr. For an inscribed/circumscribed geometry question, identify radius, centre, and any right-angle or tangent relationships explicitly.

### Common student pitfalls
Using diameter instead of radius in the area formula; confusing circumference with area.
