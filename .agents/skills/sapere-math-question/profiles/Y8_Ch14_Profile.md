# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 14
## Circles

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 2 sections 14A–14D: circle features, circumference C=2πr=πd, area A=πr², and composite figures. For sectors, take the same fraction of the full circumference as the central angle is of 360°. For annuli, subtract inner-circle area from outer-circle area.

Common errors: using diameter as radius; forgetting the two radii in a sector perimeter; subtracting circumferences when area is requested; and rounding before the final step.
