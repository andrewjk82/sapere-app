# Y11 Extension 1 — Chapter 6: Further graphs

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 11 (2nd ed.), sections 6C and 6F–6G.

## Core methods
- Reciprocal graphs: zeros of `f` become vertical asymptotes of `1/f`; analyze signs and limits near original vertical asymptotes rather than reciprocating them mechanically. Sketch intercepts, asymptotes, signs and end behaviour.
- Inverse functions: restrict to a one-to-one domain, interchange x and y, reflect in `y=x`, and swap domain/range. Check endpoint values and principal inverse-function range.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not assume an asymptote of `f` becomes a zero of `1/f` without checking the limit.
- State the restricted domain when finding an inverse.
