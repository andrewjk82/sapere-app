# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 12
## Congruent triangles

### Standard solution methodology
Triangle congruence can be established with SSS, SAS, ASA/AAS, or RHS when the matching conditions are satisfied. For a proof, name corresponding angles/sides and state the shared side or radii.

### Common student pitfalls
Using SSA as a congruence test; matching non-corresponding sides; assuming a diagram alone proves equality.
