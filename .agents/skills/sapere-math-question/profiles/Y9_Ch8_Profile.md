# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 8 — Index Laws

## Core concepts used in this batch
- Product of like bases: `a^m × a^n = a^(m+n)`.
- Quotient of like bases: `a^m ÷ a^n = a^(m−n)` for `a ≠ 0`.
- Zero index: `a^0 = 1` for `a ≠ 0`.
- Power of a power: `(a^m)^n = a^(mn)`; a power applies to every factor inside parentheses.

## Solution method
1. Identify the base and the operation connecting the powers.
2. Apply the matching index law without changing the base.
3. Simplify coefficients and combine like bases separately.
4. Check that the final expression is in the requested form and has positive indices if requested.

## Common pitfalls
- Adding exponents when dividing, or subtracting them when multiplying.
- Applying an outside power only to the variable and not to the coefficient.
- Treating `a^0` as zero rather than one.
- Combining powers with different bases.
