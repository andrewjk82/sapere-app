# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 5 — Exponential and logarithmic functions

## Core concepts
- \(rac{d}{dx}\ln f(x)=f'(x)/f(x)\).
- \(rac{d}{dx}\log_a f(x)=f'(x)/(f(x)\ln a)\).
- Exponential/logarithmic models preserve domain restrictions and often use the chain rule.

## Standard methodology
1. Identify the logarithm base and its inner function.
2. Apply the logarithmic derivative rule with the inner derivative.
3. Simplify algebraically and state any required domain restrictions.

- For base \(a
e e\), include the factor \(\ln a\) in the denominator.
- Do not omit the derivative of the logarithm's inner expression.
