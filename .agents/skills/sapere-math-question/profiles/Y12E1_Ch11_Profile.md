# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 11: Trigonometric equations

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, sections 11A–11C.

## Core methods
- Apply compound-angle identities or the t-formula. Set `t=tan θ` (or the required half-angle substitution), solve the resulting algebraic equation, and map roots back into the given angular interval.
- Check candidate solutions in the original equation.

## Common pitfalls
- The substitution may exclude angles where tangent is undefined.
- Adjust inverse-tangent values to the required quadrant and interval.
