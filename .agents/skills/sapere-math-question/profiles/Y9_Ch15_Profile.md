# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 15 — Further Factorisation

## Core concepts used in this batch
- **15B Grouping:** group terms to expose a shared factor, then factor the common bracket.
- **15D General quadratic:** factor `ax²+bx+c` by finding factor pairs whose product is `ac` and sum is `b`, then split the middle term and group.
- **15E Algebraic fractions:** factor numerator/denominator, then cancel common factors; for division, multiply by the reciprocal.
- **15F Adding/subtracting algebraic fractions:** find the lowest common denominator, rewrite each fraction, combine numerators, then simplify.

## Solution method
1. Choose the method from the structure: grouping, quadratic factoring, multiplication/division, or addition/subtraction of fractions.
2. Factor before cancelling or grouping.
3. Show every intermediate factor or common denominator; cancel factors only, never separate terms.
4. Expand or recombine to check the result and state restrictions where a denominator could be zero.

## Common pitfalls
- Cancelling across addition, such as cancelling a factor from only one term in a numerator.
- Forgetting to multiply by the reciprocal in division.
- Using a common denominator but failing to scale every numerator.
- For grouping, failing to create identical bracket factors.
- For a general quadratic, choosing factors with the right product but the wrong sum.
