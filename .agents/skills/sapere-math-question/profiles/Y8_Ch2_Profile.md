# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 2
## Fractions and decimals

### Standard solution methodology
Common denominators for addition/subtraction; multiply numerators and denominators then simplify; percent-to-fraction conversion by dividing by 100.

### Common student pitfalls
Adding numerators before matching denominators; multiplying fractions by cross-adding; failing to simplify.
