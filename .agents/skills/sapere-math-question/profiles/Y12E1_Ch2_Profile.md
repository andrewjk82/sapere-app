# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 2: Proof by mathematical induction

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, Chapter 2.

## Core method
- State the proposition clearly, verify the base case, assume it for an arbitrary integer k, and use that assumption to prove the statement for k+1.
- For series, add exactly the next term and simplify to the required expression at k+1.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not skip the base case or assume the conclusion in the inductive step.
- Keep the induction assumption distinct from the expression being proved at k+1.
