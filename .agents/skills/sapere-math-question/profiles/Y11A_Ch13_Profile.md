# Chapter Profile: Year 11 Advanced Chapter 13 — Data and probability

## Core concepts
- A discrete probability distribution has non-negative probabilities that sum to 1.
- For a continuous density, total area over its support is 1; probabilities are definite integrals of the density.
- A mode is the input where the density is greatest.

## Standard methodology
1. Read the support and identify the random variable/event.
2. Use \(\sum P(X=x)=1\) to find unknown table constants.
3. For cumulative events, add the relevant outcomes without including values outside the event.
4. Check probabilities lie in \([0,1]\) and state the requested event clearly.

## Pitfalls
- “At most 2” includes both 1 and 2.
- Unknown probability constants must be substituted into all affected entries.
- Do not confuse a score's value with its probability.
