# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 6
## Algebra – part 1

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 1 sections 6A–6G: substitution, linear equations, expanding brackets, equations with brackets, collecting like terms, algebraic problem-solving, and multiplying/dividing algebraic fractions. Show substitution with brackets where signs matter. For equations, perform the same operation on both sides and check the result. For algebraic fractions, factor, cancel common factors only, and state restrictions when relevant.

Solution flow: identify the operation or structure; write the equivalent algebraic step; simplify one operation at a time; check by substitution. Common errors: cancelling terms across addition, losing negative signs, and treating unlike terms as like terms.
