# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 6
## Substitution and equations

### Standard solution methodology
Substitute values with brackets; preserve operation order. Solve linear equations by applying inverse operations to both sides. Reverse the inequality sign when multiplying or dividing by a negative.

### Common student pitfalls
Dropping brackets around negative substitutions; changing only one side of an equation; forgetting to reverse an inequality.
