# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 7
## Percentages

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 1 sections 7A–7H: converting fractions and percentages, percentage sums/products, simple interest, discount, percentage increase/decrease, unitary method, and simple interest II. For a percentage of an amount, convert the percentage to a decimal or fraction and multiply. For simple interest use I=Prt with rate and time in matching units; total amount is P+I. For tiered rates, calculate each band separately before adding.

Common errors: applying a rate to the whole amount when it is tiered; using compound growth in a simple-interest problem; and confusing the interest with the final balance.
