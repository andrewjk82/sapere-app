# Chapter Profile: Cambridge Mathematics Extension 1 Year 11, Chapter 1
## Algebra review

### Core concepts

- Treat a pronumeral as a number and apply the distributive law to every term inside brackets.
- Collect only like terms after expanding.
- Recognise and use the special expansions: \((A+B)^2\), \((A-B)^2\), and \((A+B)(A-B)\).
- For factoring, first look for a highest common factor, then test for difference of squares, quadratic factors, or grouping.

### Standard solution method

1. Identify the factor outside the bracket and every term inside it.
2. Distribute the outside factor to every term, including its sign.
3. Collect like terms only after every product has been written.
4. State the simplified result and check that substituting a simple value gives the same value before and after expansion.

### Common student pitfalls

- Applying a negative factor to only the first term instead of every term.
- Losing the sign when subtracting a bracket.
- Combining unlike terms, such as \(x\) and \(x^2\).
- Stopping before the resulting expression is simplified.
