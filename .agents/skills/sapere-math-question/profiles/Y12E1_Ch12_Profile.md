# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 12: Further calculus

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, sections 12A–12F.

## Core methods
- Use inverse-trigonometric derivative rules, trigonometric identities, substitution, and definite integration.
- For substitution in a definite integral, transform the bounds or substitute back before evaluation.
- Volumes of revolution use disks or washers: identify the outer and inner radii at each integration value.
- Inverse functions require a stated domain and range.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not confuse radius with diameter or omit the inner washer radius.
- Transform bounds consistently and include constants of integration for indefinite integrals.
