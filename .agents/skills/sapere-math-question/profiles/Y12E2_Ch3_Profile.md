# Y12 Extension 2 — Chapter 3: Complex Numbers II — de Moivre and Euler

Source basis: local Extension 2 curriculum map (Topics 3A–3F) and Abbotsleigh 2025 4U Trial with supplied solutions.

Topics: powers, trigonometric identities, complex roots, Euler form, applications of exponential form, and review.

## Core methods
- Convert complex numbers to modulus–argument or exponential form before taking powers; use de Moivre's theorem and reduce arguments modulo 2π.
- Find nth roots by dividing the argument family by n and listing all n distinct roots.
- Derive trigonometric identities from z^n and reciprocal/conjugate relations, then compare real and imaginary parts.
- Use roots of unity as equally spaced points on the unit circle for regular polygon geometry.

## Common pitfalls
- Keep all roots in the required argument interval and include every branch.
- Distinguish z^n from n z and reduce the argument only after multiplying it by n.
- Track signs when comparing imaginary parts.
