# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 10
## Rates and Ratios

### 1. Core Concepts & Formulas

**10A & 10B: The Unitary Method**
- The unitary method involves finding the value of a single unit (1 part) before calculating the value of multiple units.
- Used for direct rates (e.g., cost per item) and reverse operations (e.g., finding the original price given a discounted price, like 80% = $64 \(\rightarrow\) 20% = $16 \(\rightarrow\) 100% = $80).
- **Changing units:** Successive one-step conversions (e.g., converting $18/hour to cents/second by first converting to cents/hour, then cents/minute, then cents/second).

**10C: Speed**
- **Speed:** A measure of how fast something is travelling.
- **Formulas:**
  - \(\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance travelled}}{\text{time taken}}\)
  - \(\text{distance travelled} = \text{speed} \times \text{time taken}\)
  - \(\text{time taken} = \frac{\text{distance travelled}}{\text{speed}}\)
- **Average speed:** Calculated over a journey where speed may vary, using total distance divided by total time.

**10D: Ratios**
- **Definition:** Compares two or more related quantities (e.g., 5:7).
- **Ratios and Fractions:** If a mixture is in the ratio \(a:b\), the total number of parts is \(a+b\). The fractions of the whole are \(\frac{a}{a+b}\) and \(\frac{b}{a+b}\).
- **Simplest form:** Divide all terms in the ratio by their highest common factor (HCF).
- **Fractions in Ratios:** To eliminate fractions in a ratio (e.g., \(\frac{1}{2} : \frac{2}{3}\)), multiply by the lowest common denominator.

**10E: Using Ratios in Problems (Dividing a Quantity)**
- **Methodology:**
  1. Add the parts of the ratio together to find the total number of parts.
  2. Divide the total quantity by the total parts to find the value of "1 part".
  3. Multiply the value of 1 part by the required ratio numbers to find each share.

**10F: Scale Drawings**
- **Definition:** \(\text{Scale} = \text{length on the drawing} : \text{length on the actual object}\)
- **Simplifying Scales:** Convert both measurements to the same unit so the scale can be written as a unitless ratio (e.g., \(1\text{ cm} : 4\text{ m} \rightarrow 1\text{ cm} : 400\text{ cm} \rightarrow 1:400\)).

### 2. Standard Solution Methodology (Step-by-Step)

When generating `solutionSteps` for this chapter, enforce the following pedagogical flow based on the topic:

**For Unitary Method & Rates:**
- **Step 1:** State the known relationship (e.g., "12 mangoes cost $24").
- **Step 2:** Find the value of 1 unit by dividing (e.g., "1 mango costs $2").
- **Step 3:** Multiply to find the target amount (e.g., "7 mangoes cost $14").
- **Step 4:** State the final answer with correct units.

**For Ratios & Dividing Quantities:**
- **Step 1:** State the given ratio and find the total number of parts by adding them (e.g., \(4 + 3 + 3 = 10\) parts).
- **Step 2:** Find the value of 1 part using division (e.g., \(\frac{\$360,000}{10} = \$36,000\)).
- **Step 3:** Multiply the value of 1 part by the required ratio numbers to find the specific shares.
- **Step 4:** State the final answer clearly in context.

**For Scale Drawings:**
- **Step 1:** State the scale (e.g., \(1\text{ cm} : 500,000\text{ cm}\)).
- **Step 2:** Convert the scale to practical units if necessary (e.g., "1 cm represents 5 km").
- **Step 3:** Multiply or divide the given measurement by the scale factor.
- **Step 4:** State the actual or drawn distance with correct units.

### 3. Common Student Pitfalls (오개념 & 잦은 실수)

Include these in the explanation of the relevant steps to preempt student errors:

- **Time Conversions in Speed:** Students often misinterpret decimals of an hour. For example, assuming \(4.5\) hours is 4 hours and 50 minutes, rather than 4 hours and 30 minutes.
  *Warning phrasing: "시간을 계산할 때 $0.5$시간은 50분이 아니라 60분의 절반인 30분임에 주의하세요."*
- **Ratios vs. Fractions:** When converting a ratio like 2:7 to a fraction, students mistakenly write \(\frac{2}{7}\) instead of adding the parts (\(2+7=9\)) to get \(\frac{2}{9}\).
  *Warning phrasing: "비율(Ratio)을 전체에 대한 분수로 바꿀 때는 반드시 전체 비율의 합(예: $2+7=9$)을 분모로 사용해야 합니다."*
- **Scale Drawing Units:** Students often remove units without matching them first, incorrectly assuming a scale of \(1\text{ cm} : 2\text{ m}\) means a ratio of \(1:2\) instead of \(1:200\).
  *Warning phrasing: "축척을 단위 없는 비율로 나타낼 때는 반드시 양쪽의 단위를 똑같이 맞춘 후(예: m를 cm로 변환) 단위를 지워야 합니다."*
- **Flipping Division in Unitary Method:** Dividing the wrong way round (e.g., finding items per dollar when dollars per item is needed).
  *Warning phrasing: "단위 값(1 part)을 구할 때는 항상 전체 수량을 전체 조각 수로 정확히 나누어야 합니다."*
