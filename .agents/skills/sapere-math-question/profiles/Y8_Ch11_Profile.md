# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 11
## Algebra part 2

### Standard solution methodology
Distribute a factor to every term in a bracket; multiply coefficients and add exponents for like bases; combine only like terms.

### Common student pitfalls
Multiplying just the first term in a bracket; adding exponents on unlike bases; confusing 2x×3x with 5x.
