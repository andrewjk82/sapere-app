# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 11
## Algebra – part 2

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 2 sections 11A–11D: expanding brackets and collecting like terms, adding and subtracting algebraic fractions, solving linear equations, and equation word problems. For algebraic fractions, form a common denominator for addition/subtraction and simplify only after combining numerators. For equations, preserve equality by applying the same operation to both sides; clear denominators using the least common denominator when helpful.

Solution flow: identify the expression/equation structure; show each equivalent transformation; collect like terms; isolate the unknown; substitute back to check. Common errors: cancelling across addition, losing a negative sign while expanding, and multiplying only one term by a denominator.
