# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 17
## Formulas and factorisation

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 2 sections 17A–17D: substitute values into formulas, expand brackets and binomial products, factorise by common factor, difference of squares, grouping, and simple quadratics. Keep each distributive product visible before collecting like terms.

Common errors: multiplying only the first term in a bracket; losing the middle sign in a difference of squares; and choosing factors whose product is right but sum is wrong.
