# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 17
## Formulas and factorisation

### Standard solution methodology
Substitute numerical values into formulas using brackets; factor out the greatest common factor; expand binomials term by term.

### Common student pitfalls
Substituting only part of a formula; missing a negative sign or middle product when expanding.
