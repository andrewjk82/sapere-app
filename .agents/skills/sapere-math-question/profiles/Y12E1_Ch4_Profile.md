# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 4: Applications of calculus

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, Chapter 4.

## Core methods
- Locate extrema by analysing critical points and domain endpoints, then report the associated coordinates and values.
- When calculus is restricted, use identities or bounds to find the largest and smallest possible values and map them back to the requested function.

## Common pitfalls
- For a positive fraction, a larger denominator gives a smaller value.
- Check the full stated domain and distinguish maximum/minimum values from their x-coordinates.
