# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 9: Related rates

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, Chapter 9.

## Core method
- Draw and label the geometric relationship, write an equation linking the changing quantities, differentiate implicitly with respect to time, and substitute the instantaneous values afterwards.
- State units and interpret the sign: a negative derivative indicates a decreasing length, while a rate “drawn in” is its positive speed.

## Common pitfalls
- Keep fixed dimensions constant when differentiating.
- Do not substitute the snapshot values before differentiating; avoid confusing a signed rate with its requested magnitude.
