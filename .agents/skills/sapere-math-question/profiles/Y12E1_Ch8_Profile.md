# Y12 Extension 1 — Chapter 8: Vectors

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 12 textbook, sections 8A–8F.

## Core methods
- Resolve vectors into components and track direction and sign.
- Use `a·b=|a||b|cosθ` for scalar products and perpendicularity.
- For vector geometry proofs, express sides by vector addition and use equal lengths or dot products as required.
- Physical vector problems: resolve velocities into along-track and cross-track components before finding resultant speed or time.

## Common pitfalls
- Distinguish a vector from its magnitude.
- Preserve direction signs when adding components.
