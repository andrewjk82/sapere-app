# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 7 — Geometry and congruence

## Core concepts
- **Angle relationships:** angles on a straight line sum to 180°, angles around a point sum to 360°, vertically opposite angles are equal, and angles in a triangle sum to 180°.
- **Parallel lines:** corresponding angles and alternate interior angles are equal; co-interior angles sum to 180°.
- **Regular polygons:** each interior angle is \(\frac{(n-2)180^\circ}{n}\); all sides and all interior angles are equal.
- **Isosceles triangles:** equal sides have equal opposite angles; the three angles total 180°.
- **Congruent triangles:** use matching side and angle information, including RHS for right-angled triangles, and state the correspondence and reason.

## Standard solution methodology
1. Mark the relevant vertices, parallel lines, equal sides, right angles, and the target angle on a clear diagram.
2. Name the exact angle rule or congruence criterion before applying it.
3. Substitute the given angle or side values and show each arithmetic step.
4. For a proof, list the matching facts in corresponding order and state the criterion that completes the proof.
5. Check that the result is consistent with the diagram and all angles in a triangle sum to 180°.

## Common student pitfalls
- Using the wrong angle sector at a vertex or confusing interior and exterior angles.
- Treating corresponding and alternate angles as equal without first identifying the parallel lines and transversal.
- Forgetting to subtract a polygon's interior angles from 360° when finding the angle between two polygons.
- Assuming an isosceles triangle's equal sides or equal angles without identifying the matching sides.
- Naming triangles in an order that does not match the equal vertices.
- For RHS, omitting that both triangles are right-angled or that the equal side is the hypotenuse.

## Diagram requirements
Show each vertex label and relevant side/angle mark clearly. Draw angle arcs inside the intended sector, with the angle value between the rays. For parallel-line questions, mark the parallel pair and highlight the transversal and alternate/corresponding angles in the worked steps. For congruence proofs, show right-angle squares, matching side ticks, and the shared hypotenuse on the exact corresponding triangles.
