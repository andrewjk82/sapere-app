# Chapter Profile: Year 11 Advanced Chapter 9 — Differentiation

## Core concepts
- Power rule: \(rac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}\); chain rule: \(rac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))g'(x)\).
- Product rule: \((uv)'=u'v+uv'\).
- The derivative is the gradient of a tangent and the instantaneous rate of change.
- Differentiability at a join requires continuity and equal one-sided derivatives.

## Standard methodology
1. Identify the outer/inner function or the two product factors.
2. Write the relevant rule explicitly before substituting.
3. Differentiate each factor, include inner derivatives, then simplify.
4. For a piecewise join, impose continuity first, then equate left and right derivatives.

## Pitfalls
- The chain rule requires multiplying by the derivative of the inner expression.
- In a product, both factors contribute a term.
- Continuity alone does not guarantee differentiability at a join.
