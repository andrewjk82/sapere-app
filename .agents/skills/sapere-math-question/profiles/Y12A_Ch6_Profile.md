# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 6 — The trigonometric functions

## Core concepts
- \((\sin u)'=u'\cos u\), \((\cos u)'=-u'\sin u\), and \((	an u)'=u'\sec^2u\).
- Antiderivatives: \(\int f'(x)\sin f(x)dx=-\cos f(x)+C\), and \(\int f'(x)\cos f(x)dx=\sin f(x)+C\).
- For a tangent at \(x=a\), use point \((a,f(a))\) and gradient \(f'(a)\).

## Standard methodology
1. Differentiate/integrate the outer trigonometric function and include the inner derivative.
2. Substitute the given angle/point only after forming the general derivative.
3. Use point-gradient form for tangents and simplify the final equation.
4. For integrals, verify by differentiating the result.

## Pitfalls
- The chain rule factor is essential for composite angles.
- The derivative of cosine has a negative sign.
- In tangent equations, use radians when the calculus convention is radians.
