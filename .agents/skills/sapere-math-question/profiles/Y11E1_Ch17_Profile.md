# Y11 Extension 1 — Chapter 17: Combinatorics

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 11 (2nd ed.), sections 17A–17E.

## Core methods
- **Binomial theorem (17B):** use \(T_{r+1}=\binom nr a^{n-r}b^r\), with the first term corresponding to \(r=0\).
- For a block arrangement, treat the required group as one object, arrange all objects, then arrange members inside the block.
- Divide by factorial multiplicities when objects are identical.
- Distinguish ordered arrangements from unordered selections; combinations ignore order.

## Common pitfalls
- Include internal permutations of the block.
- Do not use permutations when order is irrelevant.
- Count terms from the first term carefully and preserve the sign from an odd power of a negative binomial term.
