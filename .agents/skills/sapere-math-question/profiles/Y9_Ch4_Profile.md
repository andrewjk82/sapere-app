# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 4 — Factorisation

## Core concepts
- **4A Common factors:** factor out the highest common numerical and algebraic factor.
- **4B Difference of two squares:** `a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)`.
- **4C Simple quadratics:** find two numbers whose product is the constant term and whose sum is the coefficient of the linear term; write the two binomial factors.

## Solution method
1. Check whether every term has a common factor.
2. If there are two squared terms being subtracted, use the difference-of-squares identity.
3. For a monic quadratic, identify the required product and sum, then form the brackets.
4. Expand the result to verify that it reproduces the original expression.

## Common pitfalls
- Leaving a common factor inside the brackets or omitting a term.
- Using a sum-of-squares expression as though it were a difference of squares.
- Choosing factor numbers with the right product but the wrong sum/signs.
