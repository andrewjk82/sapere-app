# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 4
## Integers

### Standard solution methodology
For multiplication or division, numbers with opposite signs give a negative result. When substituting a negative value, use brackets before applying powers or subtraction.

### Common student pitfalls
Treating a negative input as positive; confusing subtraction of a negative with subtracting a positive.
