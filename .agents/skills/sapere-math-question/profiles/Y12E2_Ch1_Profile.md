# Y12 Extension 2 — Chapter 1: Complex Numbers I

Source basis: local Extension 2 curriculum map (Topics 1A–1G) and Abbotsleigh 2025 4U Trial with supplied solutions.

Topics: arithmetic, quadratic equations, Argand diagram, modulus-argument form, vectors in the complex plane, Argand curves and regions, and complex polynomial roots.

## Core methods
- Perform arithmetic using i² = −1; for division multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate.
- Solve complex quadratics with the quadratic formula and express square roots of complex numbers as a+ib by matching real and imaginary parts.
- Represent z=x+iy on an Argand diagram; interpret arguments as directed angles and compare them with attention to principal argument and equal-angle loci.
- Use modulus–argument form when multiplication, division, powers, or geometric transformations are involved.
- Use conjugate roots for polynomials with real coefficients.

## Common pitfalls
- Apply the principal argument consistently and avoid confusing equal arguments with supplementary angles.
- When dividing by a complex number, multiply both numerator and denominator by its conjugate.
- For real-coefficient polynomials, include conjugate roots and check all coefficients.
