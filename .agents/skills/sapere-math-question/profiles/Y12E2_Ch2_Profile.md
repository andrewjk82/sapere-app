# Y12 Extension 2 — Chapter 2: Nature of Proof

Source basis: local Extension 2 curriculum map (2A–2G) and Abbotsleigh 2025 Trial with supplied solutions.

## Core methods
- Translate implications and quantified statements precisely; form negations by switching quantifiers and negating predicates.
- Prove an implication by its contrapositive when parity or divisibility becomes straightforward.
- For induction, establish the base case, assume the statement for n=k, and derive it for k+1.
- Use definitions and ordered inequalities to prove algebraic inequalities; preserve the direction when multiplying by positive quantities.
- A counterexample disproves a universal claim; compare total distance divided by total time for average speed.

## Common pitfalls
- The converse is not generally equivalent to an implication; only the contrapositive is.
- Include the induction hypothesis explicitly and finish with a quantified conclusion.
- Do not mistake an average of speeds for total distance / total time.
