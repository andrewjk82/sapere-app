# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 19
## Statistics

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 2 sections 19A–19C: organise data in stem-and-leaf plots; compare means and medians; consider the effect of extreme values; and interpret samples. The median requires ordered data; for an even count average the two central values. The mean is total divided by count. Show sorted data and arithmetic clearly.

Common errors: finding a median before sorting; using only one central value when there are an even number of data; and confusing mean with median.
