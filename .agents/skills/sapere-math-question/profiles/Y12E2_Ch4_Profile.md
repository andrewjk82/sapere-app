# Y12 Extension 2 — Chapter 4: Integration

Source basis: local Extension 2 curriculum map (Topics 4A–4G) and Abbotsleigh 2025 4U Trial with supplied solutions.

Topics: standard integrals, algebraic manipulation, substitution, partial fractions, quadratic denominators, integration by parts, and trigonometric integrals.

## Core methods
- Choose a substitution that simplifies the integrand and transform dx and both bounds consistently.
- Decompose proper rational functions into partial fractions before integrating; integrate each logarithmic term with absolute values when needed.
- Use integration by parts for logarithmic factors and trigonometric identities/symmetry for definite integrals.
- Derive and apply reduction formulae with explicit boundary terms.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not lose the negative sign from reversing bounds or from dx under a substitution.
- Keep the factor from the chain rule when integrating trig functions.
- Verify endpoint terms before applying a recurrence.
