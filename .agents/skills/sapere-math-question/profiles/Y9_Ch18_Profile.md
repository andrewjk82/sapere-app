# Chapter Profile: Year 9 Chapter 18 — Rates and Direct Proportion

## Core concepts used in this batch
- A rate compares two quantities measured in different units, such as dollars per hour.
- Amount earned at a constant rate: `total = rate × time`.
- For a repeated schedule, calculate one period first, then multiply by the number of periods.
- Keep time units consistent (hours, days, weeks, years) before multiplying.

## Solution method
1. Identify the rate and the time interval it applies to.
2. Convert the given time to compatible units if needed.
3. Multiply rate by time and show the intermediate unit (e.g. dollars per day).
4. Apply the number of days/weeks only after finding the per-period amount; include currency and check scale.

## Common pitfalls
- Counting elapsed time incorrectly, especially from morning to afternoon.
- Multiplying by 52 weeks but forgetting five workdays per week.
- Mixing hours and minutes without converting.
- Confusing a rate with the total amount earned.
