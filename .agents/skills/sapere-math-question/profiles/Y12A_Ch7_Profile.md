# Chapter Profile: Year 12 Advanced Chapter 7 — Motion and rates

## Core concepts
- Displacement derivative is velocity; velocity derivative is acceleration.
- The signed area under a velocity-time graph is change in displacement.
- Return to origin means net signed displacement from the initial position is zero.

## Standard methodology
1. Identify whether the given function is position, velocity, or acceleration.
2. Differentiate/integrate as needed and keep the time domain.
3. For return-to-origin questions, set cumulative signed area/displacement equal to zero, splitting at piecewise boundaries.
4. Solve and check the time lies in the correct interval; report all intervals where the requested condition holds.

## Pitfalls
- Distance travelled is not the same as signed displacement.
- Check piecewise formulas at their boundary and do not integrate the wrong branch.
- A particle is at rest only when velocity is zero within the allowed time domain.
