# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 15
## Areas, Volumes and Time

### 1. Core Concepts & Formulas

**15A: Review of area and length**
- **Square**: Area = $s^2$
- **Rectangle**: Area = $lw$
- **Triangle**: Area = $\frac{1}{2}bh$
- **Composite figures**: Dissect into simpler non-overlapping figures, or subtract removed parts.

**15B: Areas of special quadrilaterals**
- **Parallelogram**: Area = $bh$ (where $b$ is base and $h$ is perpendicular height).
- **Trapezium**: Area = $\frac{1}{2}h(a + b)$ (where $a$ and $b$ are parallel sides, $h$ is perpendicular height).
- **Rhombus and Kite**: Area = $\frac{1}{2}xy$ (where $x$ and $y$ are the lengths of the diagonals).

**15C: Volume of a rectangular prism**
- **Volume**: $V = lwh$ or $V = Ah$ (Area of base $\times$ height).
- Note: The base can be any of the three different faces.

**15D: Volumes of other prisms**
- **Right prism**: A polyhedron with two congruent parallel faces (base and top) and rectangular sides. Has a uniform cross-section.
- **Volume**: $V = Ah$. Applies to triangular prisms, and any polygonal prism.

**15E: Volume of a cylinder**
- **Volume**: $V = \pi r^2 h$
- The cross-section is a circle with area $\pi r^2$.

**15F: Surface area of a prism**
- **Surface Area**: The sum of the areas of all its faces.
- **Rectangular prism**: $SA = 2(ab + ac + bc)$.
- Can be visualized using a flattened net.

**15G: Conversion of units**
- **Length**: $1\text{ cm} = 10\text{ mm}$, $1\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm}$, $1\text{ km} = 1000\text{ m}$.
- **Area**: $1\text{ cm}^2 = 100\text{ mm}^2$, $1\text{ m}^2 = 10^4\text{ cm}^2$, $1\text{ km}^2 = 10^6\text{ m}^2$.
- **Land Area**: $1\text{ ha} = 10^4\text{ m}^2$ (a square with side 100m). $1\text{ km}^2 = 100\text{ ha}$.
- **Volume**: $1\text{ cm}^3 = 10^3\text{ mm}^3$, $1\text{ m}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3$, $1\text{ km}^3 = 10^9\text{ m}^3$.
- **Capacity (Liquids)**: $1\text{ L} = 1000\text{ mL}$, $1\text{ mL} = 1\text{ cm}^3$, $1\text{ L} = 1000\text{ cm}^3$, $1\text{ m}^3 = 1000\text{ L}$.

**15H: Time**
- **Conversions**: $1\text{ min} = 60\text{ s}$, $1\text{ h} = 60\text{ min}$, $1\text{ day} = 24\text{ h}$, $1\text{ week} = 7\text{ days}$.
- **12-hour / 24-hour systems**: a.m. (ante meridiem), p.m. (post meridiem).
- **Elapsed time**: Build up to whole minutes or hours when calculating differences.

### 2. Common Pitfalls & Constraints (Category F & J Rules)

- **Unit Mismatch**: Using $cm$ and $m$ in the same calculation without converting first. Ensure all units match before applying area/volume formulas.
- **Radius vs Diameter**: In cylinders ($15E$), confusing diameter with radius. Always explicitly halve the diameter if given.
- **Base vs Parallel Sides in Trapezia**: Confusing the non-parallel sides with the parallel sides $a$ and $b$.
- **Perpendicular Height**: Using slant height instead of perpendicular height in triangles, parallelograms, and trapezia.
- **Time Arithmetic**: Adding/subtracting times using base 100 instead of base 60 (e.g., $1\text{ h } 40\text{ m} + 30\text{ m} = 1\text{ h } 70\text{ m} = 2\text{ h } 10\text{ m}$).
