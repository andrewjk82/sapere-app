# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 18
## Graphing straight lines

Use ICE-EM Year 8 Book 2 sections 18A–18H: Cartesian coordinates, straight-line graphs through or not through the origin, points and lines, gradient and y-intercept, gradients, and constant-rate applications. Gradient m=(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁); in y=mx+b, m is gradient and b is y-intercept. Parallel lines have equal gradients. A horizontal line y=c has gradient 0.

For drawing questions, provide axes as the prompt diagram, keep the finished line only in the sample answer, and show points/calculations progressively. Common errors: reversing coordinate order and reading the y-intercept as the x-intercept.
