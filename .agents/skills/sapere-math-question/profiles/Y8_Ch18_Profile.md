# Chapter Profile: Year 8 Chapter 18
## Graphing straight lines

### Standard solution methodology
In y=mx+c, m is the gradient and c is the y-intercept. Use y=mx+c with known points, and evaluate each coordinate carefully. For a right triangle on a grid, use horizontal and vertical differences.

### Common student pitfalls
Reading the x-intercept as the y-intercept; swapping rise/run; plotting the coordinate (x,y) as (y,x).
