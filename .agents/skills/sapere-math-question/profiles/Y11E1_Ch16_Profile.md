# Y11 Extension 1 — Chapter 16: Further trigonometry

Source: Cambridge Mathematics NSW Stage 6 Extension 1 Year 11 (2nd ed.), sections 16B–16E.

## Core methods
- Use compound-angle and double-angle identities, then solve exact trigonometric equations over the full stated interval.
- Rewrite `a cos x + b sin x = R cos(x−α)` with `R=√(a²+b²)` and select `α` using both coefficient signs and the correct quadrant.
- For tangent differences, use `tan(A−B)=(tan A−tan B)/(1+tan A tan B)` and check the angle domain.

## Common pitfalls
- Do not omit solutions in the interval.
- Do not choose the phase angle from `tan α` alone; confirm its quadrant.
