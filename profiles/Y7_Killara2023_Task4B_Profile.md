## Source and scope

The examination gives 55 working minutes and 57 marks: Fundamentals (Q1–11, 11 marks), Routine Processes (Q12–21, 24 marks), Application (Q22–31, 18 marks), and Challenge (Q32–33, 4 marks). ## Y7 topic map
- Q1, Q11, Q13–15, Q21, Q31: fractions and fraction operations (`y7-4` / `y7-4p2`).
- Q2, Q3, Q7, Q12, Q29: division, order of operations, multiplication, and integer-context arithmetic (`y7-1`).
- Q4, Q5, Q23: powers, divisibility, and prime factorisation (`y7-2`).
- Q6, Q19, Q20, Q27a, Q28: perimeter, area, triangle/trapezium formula, and unit conversion (`y7-8`). Q27b is grouped with the shared area task and solves the resulting equation.
- Q8, Q16, Q33: percentages and percentage of a quantity (`y7-14`).
- Q9, Q17: collecting like terms and brackets (`y7-3`).
- Q10, Q18, Q27b, Q32: one-step/multi-step and contextual equation reasoning (`y7-15`).
- Q15: recurring fractions as decimals (`y7-7`).
- Q22: mental multiplication using the distributive property (`y7-1`).
- Q24–26: algebraic notation, expressions, and expansion (`y7-3`).
- Q30: substitution into a formula (`y7-11`).
